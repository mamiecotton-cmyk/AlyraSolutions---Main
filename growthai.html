Unlike traditional SEO, GEO focuses on entity authority — making your business a recognized, trusted subject in AI knowledge systems — rather than chasing keyword rankings.

  • AI engines pull from citation consistency across directories, structured data on your website, editorial coverage on third-party platforms, review velocity, and your overall entity authority. Businesses with complete, consistent, well-cited profiles are far more likely to appear in AI-generated recommendations.

  • AI visibility is entirely organic — there is no ad slot to buy inside a ChatGPT or Gemini answer. You either have the authority signals to be recommended, or you don't. GEO builds those signals systematically, creating a durable competitive advantage that compounds over time without ongoing ad spend.
