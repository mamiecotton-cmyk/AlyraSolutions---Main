Questions about AI visibility, pricing, platforms, and how Alyra works — answered directly.

  • GEO is the practice of optimizing your business to be discovered and recommended by AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. When someone asks an AI "best Italian restaurant in Silver Lake" or "top nail salon near me," those answers are generated from signals your business either has or doesn't. GEO closes that gap. Unlike traditional SEO, GEO focuses on entity authority — making your business a recognized, trusted subject in AI knowledge systems — rather than chasing keyword rankings on a results page.

  • AI engines pull from a combination of signals: citation consistency across directories, structured data on your website, editorial coverage on third-party platforms, review velocity, and the overall authority of your online entity. Businesses with complete, consistent, and well-cited profiles across the web are far more likely to appear in AI-generated recommendations. Most independent service businesses have significant gaps in these areas — which is exactly the opportunity Alyra is designed to close.

  • Traditional SEO targets blue-link search results on Google. Paid ads require ongoing spend and disappear the moment you stop paying. AI visibility is entirely organic — there is no ad slot to buy inside a ChatGPT or Gemini answer. You either have the authority signals to be recommended, or you don't. GEO builds those signals systematically, creating a durable competitive advantage that compounds over time and does not require a media budget.

  • GrowthAI is built for service-based businesses that depend on local discovery — primarily upscale independent restaurants, nail salons, spas, and barbershops. These categories are ideal because customers search for them by type and location constantly, and AI-generated answers are already influencing booking decisions. If your revenue depends on new and repeat guests finding you, AI visibility directly affects your bottom line.

Ascend adds authority building, editorial outreach, competitor GEO gap analysis, and bi-weekly strategy calls.

Authority building and editorial outreach compounds over a longer arc. Alyra tracks your AI engine scores monthly so you can see the lift numerically, not just anecdotally. The GEO Audit report shows your current score and a projected 90-day target so expectations are grounded in data from day one.

You get a full picture of where you stand and exactly what needs to happen to improve it.
